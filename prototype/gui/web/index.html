fvTrace prototype

Three designs show the same claim trace. The trace is a hand-written fixture of the tau × amyloid microglia study.

  • Claim ledger: what you can say, what you cannot say, and what verification would change.
  • Dependency map: questions, claims and the obligations below them, as a graph.
  • Manuscript check: a draft results paragraph with each overstated sentence marked.

Prototype: statuses come from the fixture, not from a live check. No analysis runs. What-if changes are hypothetical and are not saved.
